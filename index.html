    Illustrative model force-displacement

    Positive compression shortens the specimen. Positive force is the compressive actuator reaction. The amber path is loading. The dashed path is unloading. It ends at the shortened zero-load state. That open path is not a closed reset cycle, and its area is not dissipated energy. The drawn curve is a short display average of the model samples. The numeric actuator force is the instantaneous value.

    Model energy units, fixed scale. External work, elastic energy, kinetic energy, and illustrative material damping are separate. Cyan on the specimen marks State B. It does not encode an energy value.

    About this explainer

    This is a cinematic viewer of a four-row series array of conceptually bistable curved cells. One precomputed trajectory drives the geometry, the force chart, and the energy readouts.

    1. Product: a 34-second interactive explainer of elastic snap-through and retained configuration.
    2. Audience: engineers and students inspecting the mechanism, not buyers of an impact product.
    3. Distinction: unloading stops at the higher-energy zero-load state, and reset is a separate actuated cell.
    4. Structure: five shots, a 3D specimen, an unloaded reference landscape, an open force path, and an inset.
    5. Discovery: open the page directly. A public Pages URL is not claimed until a deployment is verified.
    6. Engagement: play, pause, scrub, mute, and step through shots. Reduced motion keeps the camera on shot poses.
    7. Conversion: none. The page does not collect leads or personal data.
    8. Money: none on this page. It is a technical demonstration.
    9. Launch: local build and preview. Remote publication waits for an approved repository target.
    10. After launch: replace the reduced-order model only with a documented experiment or analysis. Do not relabel this run as validation.
